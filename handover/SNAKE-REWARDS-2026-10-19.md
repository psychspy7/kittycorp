# Snake rewards — 19 October 2026

Replaces Circuit Courier at /play with a 20×20 Snake game. Each food is one point. Movement starts at 210ms per step and decreases by 2ms per point, to 70ms at 70 points. 50–69 points earns 5%; 70 earns 7% and ends the round. A player can pause and claim at 50. A crash preserves an earned tier. Arrow keys, WASD and touch direction buttons work; Space, explicit controls, window blur and hidden-tab handling pause. A signed round expires after 20 minutes, including pauses and follow confirmation.

The server calculates the score by replaying the seeded game, checking bounds, body collision, reversal and terminal state. It accepts a bounded direction string (20,000 ticks, below the existing 32KB request limit), never a client score/percentage. Signed visitor-bound challenges now use version 2 / kitty-snake-v2, rejecting old maze sessions. Previously issued reward codes keep their original HMAC format and remain redeemable. Existing private reward ledger, quotas, 30-day validity, 5→7 upgrade, no stacking and Studio redemption are unchanged. No database migration or provider/environment change is required. Deterministic replay is validation, not proof of human play or complete bot prevention; existing Turnstile remains on start.

Before claiming, visitors open Instagram, X and LinkedIn links and tick a self-reported follow confirmation for each. The API requires all three confirmations. No social OAuth/permission or real follow verification exists, and no social handles are recorded. The UI and privacy page state this explicitly. Existing Studio company social fields supply links with HTTPS/host validation. Fallbacks are the existing kittycorpHQ Instagram, KittyCorphq X and the previously supplied Virat Anand LinkedIn profile, labelled as the founder's profile. Changing social links in Studio updates the page through the normal content cache. The follow condition is disclosed before playing. No accounts are followed on the owner's behalf.
